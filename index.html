Pipeline concept

A future interaction model for moving saved programmes through review, preparation, submission, and outcome stages.

Information first

The prototype prioritises deadline precision, eligibility, expected workload, and exactly what funding covers before visual urgency.

Prototype boundaries

  • No live database or organiser integrations
  • No accounts or private application materials
  • No claim that sample cards are real opportunities

Design notes

Built as a compact frontend exercise around card hierarchy, filtering, responsive layouts, and evidence-first opportunity research.
